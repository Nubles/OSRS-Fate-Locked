# RuneProof simpler guide

Approved by the maintainer on 23 September 2026 after reviewing the current guide.

## Player experience

The guide answers whether the player can begin or continue, then presents one numbered walkthrough in its reviewed order. Each step keeps its instruction, recognizable location, small map link, necessary items, and relevant unmet requirements together. Completed checks and the next action remain clear. Remove repeated positive travel/destination badges, the contents sidebar, duplicate destination/transit inventories, and the quick/detailed mode switch.

A collapsed Step details disclosure holds additional reviewed prose and the named walking path. Quest details and rewards, alternative sources, coordinate display, and evidence remain available after the walkthrough. The readiness message must not imply full completion is possible merely because the first action is available. The Restless Ghost may start at Neck T0, but finishing requires Neck T1; the warning belongs beside the step that requires wearing the amulet.

## Preserved behavior

Keep the reviewed sources, guide revision guard, promoted alternative locations, account-derived travel checks, equipment restrictions, run-scoped checks, Undo, and map focus restoration. Simplifying presentation must never loosen confirmation guards or update Journal completion. Do not infer missing travel or quest facts.

## Verification

Test and visually verify Vanilla only, in the separate Vanilla Preview profile. Check Restless Ghost at Neck T0 and T1, maps, details, completion guards, saved checks/Undo, and narrow screens. Type checking and a production build remain required. Chunked remains experimental and is outside this verification scope. No public deployment is requested.
