# Audit repairs — 22 September 2026

All **63 confirmed audit groups** from baseline `ce55716bfbf43bc73583e1efae70db2bdebf078d` are addressed on `codex/fix-audit-findings`. This repairs the reported issues; it does not certify every remaining app datum or undocumented OSRS mechanic. Changes have not been deployed.

## Verification

Final integrated validation passed: **267 test files / 3,517 tests**, TypeScript, the complete content/source checks, player-facing changelog verification, and the production build. The first full run found 19 integration assertions covering stale reviewed fingerprints/projections, Quest Point classification, and old cache/bank/changelog expectations; those were corrected and the complete suite passed. The independent review also found and resolved the two edge cases described below.

A fresh local browser run completed onboarding and verified blocked Godsword ingredients, raid-only Noxifer guidance, Light versus Heavy ranged results, Augury damage, distinct Available/Locked achievement lists, and a clean Collection Log. Final rebuild/reload preserved the test save. No live-site save, real WOM account or relay was changed. Existing build notices about large bundles and Vite deprecations remain; they are not failing checks.

## Compatibility and deliberate limits

- Existing saves retain their IDs, earned rewards and integrity history. Missing historical ritual costs are explicitly estimated; those old facts cannot be reconstructed exactly.
- Existing seeded runs retain version 1. New seeded runs use version 2, which excludes generated IDs and timestamps from the random context.
- Collection Log runtime updates are detection-only. Historical identity conflicts block both manual and detected-event writes/rewards pending review; saved counts and old identity caches are preserved. Missing historical mapping evidence cannot be recreated safely.
- Partial quest progress, carried items, raid state and local entry conditions that the app cannot observe remain explicit manual checks. Unverified data is not promoted to ready.
- Combat remains the disclosed baseline model, excluding special attacks and item passives. Unknown weapon classes have a visible default and a manual ranged-type selector.
- Wise Old Man and relay side effects were verified with mocks; no real player account was refreshed and no live relay reward was granted.
