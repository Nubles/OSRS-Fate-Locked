Limitations: most Wiki pages were verified through indexed excerpts because direct fetches failed. Rogues' Den uses the legacy OSRS Wiki because the current page could not be retrieved. No account inventory, learned-spell flags, house furnishings or combined-skill predicate was added. Consequently those facts remain explicit confirmations. Legends' Guild retains completed-quest access for full guild facilities; grounds/partial-quest access is not represented as a separate activity. Other guilds' existing level/quest gates were not independently re-audited. This pass does not certify every method or equipment gate used inside a guild.

Final integration review: Arceuus access retains confirmation that the spellbook is active or a legal switching route is reachable. It is not automatically READY from ownership alone.
