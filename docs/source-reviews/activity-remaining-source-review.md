# Remaining activity requirement source review — 5 September 2026

This pass reviewed the remaining 69 legacy UNKNOWN note entries, all 19 geography-only boss/minigame entries, and adjacent clear access/use errors. The only explicitly unresolved catalog mapping is Aquarium: no verified OSRS facility was found. It stays UNKNOWN. A geography-only entry introduced in future also stays UNKNOWN. This is an access/use review, not proof that every strategy, upgrade, route, or player inventory is complete.

## Changes

- Separated use of already built POH facilities and STASH units from construction/acquisition levels. Using a facility requires confirmation that it exists, can be reached legally, supports the chosen upgrade/destination, and is permitted by the account mode. Construction levels remain informational where appropriate.
- Storage tools require the actual usable item, capacity and supported contents. Reward-source quests/levels no longer substitute for possession. Corrected Meat Pouch to Hunter storage, Tackle Box to Tempoross, colossal pouch minimum to 25 Runecraft, herb sack use to 58 Herblore.
- Farming entries retain actual species/base levels plus seed, tool, patch and method checks. Added Coral Nursery at 28 Farming after Troubled Tortugans, with selected higher species and diving route checks. Anima is 76; Vinery is 36.
- Added explicit requirements for all formerly geography-only bosses/minigames. Sarachnis needs web access equipment; Giant Mole needs a spade; Kalphite Queen requires usable descent ropes or permanent diary access; Dagannoth Kings has supported doors/shortcut alternatives. Entries with no extra hard gate retain informational notes rather than invented combat recommendations.
- Calvar'ion accepts Hard Wilderness diary OR an assigned Vet'ion boss task. Medium diary and a skeleton task do not substitute.
- Nex models the permanently opened Frozen Door, legal current route and current Zaros essence OR ecumenical key. The four general levels are frozen-key acquisition conditions, not repeated prison entry checks.
- Volcanic Mine includes Kudos, camp reward and current-entry requirements; Barbarian Assault includes its tutorial/team; Pyramid Plunder accepts quest progress instead of demanding full completion.
- Teleport items require possession and charges/destination where applicable; Digsite pendant no longer demands Bone Voyage for its basic destination; Magic Carpets do not universally require The Feud; Puro-Puro may be entered through overworld crop circles without Lost City.
- Warriors Guild uses typed combined Attack + Strength 130, including 65 + 65, alongside either individual 99 route. Prototype names cannot inherit a fake requirement from object properties.
